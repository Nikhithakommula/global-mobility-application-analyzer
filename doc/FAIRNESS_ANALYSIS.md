# Fairness Analysis

## Overview
GMAA includes group-level fairness analysis to examine whether model predictions differ across applicant groups.

## Groups Evaluated
- Job experience
- Education level
- Continent
- Employment region

## Purpose
- Compare model outcomes across different groups.
- Identify potential differences in prediction patterns.
- Highlight areas that may require further investigation.

## Responsible Use
Differences in model predictions do not automatically prove discrimination. Fairness analysis should be interpreted carefully and supported by domain expertise.

## Limitation
This analysis is exploratory and does not establish legal or statistical fairness.
