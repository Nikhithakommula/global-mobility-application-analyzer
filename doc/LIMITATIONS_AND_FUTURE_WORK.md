# Limitations and Future Improvements

## Current Limitations
- The model is trained on the EasyVisa dataset and may not generalize to all immigration systems.
- Predictions depend on the quality and coverage of the training data.
- Model outputs are estimates, not official immigration decisions.
- Fairness analysis is exploratory.
- SHAP explanations describe model behavior, not legal reasoning.

## Future Improvements
- Evaluate the model on newer and more diverse datasets.
- Add automated unit and integration tests.
- Improve model calibration and fairness evaluation.
- Expand explainability features.
- Strengthen input validation and error handling.
- Explore deployment options for wider accessibility.

## Responsible Use
GMAA is an academic decision-support prototype. Its predictions should not replace professional or authorized human judgment.
