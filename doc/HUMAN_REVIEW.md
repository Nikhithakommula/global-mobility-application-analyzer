# Human Review Mechanism

## Overview
GMAA uses confidence-based thresholds to categorize predictions and identify cases that may need human review.

## Prediction Categories

| Certified Probability | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Below 0.30 | Automatic Denied |
| 0.30 to 0.70 | Human Review |
| Above 0.70 | Automatic Certified |

## Purpose
- Flag uncertain predictions for human assessment.
- Reduce reliance on low-confidence predictions.
- Make the assessment process more transparent.

## Important Note
These thresholds are experimental project settings, not official immigration decision rules. Final decisions must be made by authorized human reviewers.
